## Findings that shape the pipeline

- **Every feed carries `title` + `link` + `pubDate`**; description/summary present
  everywhere except CNN World, which puts the summary in `content` — the
  normalizer must fall back `description → content`.
- **No feed carries a media:image** — `image_url` stays null (the reference's
  own `news_items` has mostly-null image_url too, so no parity loss).
- **Google News RSS works from this host** — no geo-block or rate-limit hit
  during probes; it also adds a `source` element (e.g. "Reuters") we can keep.
- **ZeroHedge's summary contains the full article text** (273KB for 25 items)
  — cap summary length in the normalizer (e.g. 600 chars) to keep the payload
  sane; the reference's summaries are also long-form.
- **Per-feed item counts (latest):** ZeroHedge 25, Al Jazeera 25, CNN 29,
  MarketWatch 10, Reuters-GN 100, GN top 38, CNBC 30, BP 10+10 — ~**277
  items per full sweep** before dedupe; Google News feeds dominate.
- **Dedupe key:** `link` (the reference dedupes on `url` too). Google News
  links are `news.google.com` redirect URLs — dedupe should canonicalize by
  stripping `news.google.com` redirect query params, or keep the original
  publisher URL from the `source`/link when present.
- **pubDate parsing:** RSS 2.0 RFC-822 (`Thu, 07 Aug 2026 12:00:00 +0000`) —
  parse with `email.utils.parsedate_to_datetime`; feeds with missing/bad
  dates fall back to fetch time (never fabricate a timestamp).
