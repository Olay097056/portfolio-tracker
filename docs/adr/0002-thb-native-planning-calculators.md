# DCA Projection and Passive Income calculators are THB-native, unlike per-share data

PRD section 9 fixes all per-share/per-ticker numbers (price, avg cost, P&L) as USD always, converting to THB only at the portfolio-summary level via a real FX rate. The two new planning calculators ported from stockvision-app (DCA Projection, Passive Income) deliberately break that rule: their inputs and outputs are THB, because they answer a real-world cash-flow question ("how much would I need invested to get ฿30,000/month") rather than displaying market data directly. They still source the underlying yield/growth-rate assumptions from real USD market data and convert via the same FX mechanism PRD section 9 already specifies — they do not hardcode an FX rate or a fixed dividend tax rate the way the stockvision-app draft did.
