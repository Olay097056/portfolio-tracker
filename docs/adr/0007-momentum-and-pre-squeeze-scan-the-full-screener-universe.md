# Momentum Scanner and Pre-Squeeze Scanner scan the Screener's full universe, not the Watchlist

> **Status: Reverted.** Momentum Scanner, Pre-Squeeze Scanner, and the `technical_signals`
> pipeline this ADR describes were all removed from the project shortly after this was
> written — the user decided not to keep those two tools at all. Left here as a historical
> record of the reasoning at the time, not as a description of current behavior. ADR 0004's
> Watchlist-only scoping is therefore back in effect for Dividend Ranking, the one scanner
> that still exists.

This partially supersedes ADR 0004. That ADR scoped all three signal scanners (Dividend Ranking, Momentum Scanner, Pre-Squeeze Scanner) to the Watchlist because yfinance — the only market-data source at the time — has no bulk endpoint, and scanning thousands of tickers interactively risked exactly the rate-limiting this project later hit (yfinance returned HTTP 429 during development). That constraint hasn't gone away: computing RSI, volume ratio, SMA distance, Bollinger Band width, and ATR still means one yfinance price-history fetch per ticker. What changed is that the Stock Screener (ADR 0006) now gives the app a real, whole-market ticker universe from Finnhub — the "no bulk data source" premise ADR 0004 was written around no longer holds for *discovering* tickers, even though it still holds for *fetching yfinance price history* one at a time. Momentum Scanner and Pre-Squeeze Scanner's own descriptions ("scan stocks with strong buy pressure", "scan stocks in a squeeze about to break out") are inherently about finding new opportunities across the market, not monitoring a hand-picked Watchlist — being limited to the Watchlist meant a user could only ever scan tickers they'd already decided were worth tracking, which is close to the opposite of what a momentum/squeeze scanner is for. The fix keeps the per-ticker yfinance cost exactly where ADR 0004 put it — a background job, not an interactive request per scan — but moves that job from "the user's small Watchlist, fetched live on every click" to "every Screener ticker, refreshed on demand and cached", the same pattern the Screener itself already uses for its own Finnhub data. Dividend Ranking is unaffected and stays Watchlist-scoped; this ADR only covers the two scanners whose own stated purpose is market-wide discovery.
